# Waydots Homepage Feature Brief

Use this document as the source of truth for writing a homepage for the app. Keep the copy focused on what the product does for users. Do not mention frameworks, libraries, backend services, or payment providers.

## Product Summary

Waydots is a calendar and goal-planning app that helps people see where their time goes and whether that time is actually moving them toward meaningful goals.

It is not just a place to store events. It combines scheduling, goal-setting, recurring routines, reminders, and progress tracking in one system so users can plan their time and measure the outcome.

## Core Positioning

- A calendar that turns time into measurable progress
- A planning tool built around goals, not just appointments
- A system for people who want clarity, consistency, and focus

## Main Features

### 1. Multi-view calendar planning

Users can plan and review their schedule across:

- Day view
- Week view
- Month view
- Year view

This lets them zoom in for detailed daily planning or zoom out for bigger-picture planning.

### 2. Goal-linked events

Events can be connected directly to goals. This is one of the app’s most important features.

Instead of treating goals and calendar events as separate things, Waydots connects them:

- A user creates a goal
- A user schedules events related to that goal
- Time spent on those events contributes to visible progress

This helps users answer: “Did I actually spend time on what matters?”

### 3. Goal planning across different time horizons

Users can organize goals by:

- Weekly goals
- Monthly goals
- Yearly goals
- Lifetime goals

This gives the app a layered planning structure, where short-term work connects to long-term direction.

### 4. Progress tracking and analytics

Waydots shows how much time is going toward each goal and makes progress visible instead of vague.

The app includes:

- Goal progress tracking
- Activity summaries
- Time-per-goal insights
- Visual breakdowns across day, week, month, year, and lifetime ranges
- Streak and consistency tracking

This makes the product feel more like a progress system than a basic calendar.

### 5. Recurring events and routines

Users can create repeating events for routines and habits instead of recreating them manually.

This supports:

- Repeating schedules
- Ongoing routines
- Habit-building workflows
- Better consistency over time

### 6. Event reminders

Users can set reminders before events so they do not lose track of what is coming up.

Reminder timing includes options like:

- 5 minutes before
- 10 minutes before
- 15 minutes before
- 30 minutes before
- 1 hour before
- 1 day before

### 7. Search and quick access

Users can search events and jump to them quickly, which makes the app easier to use once a schedule becomes full.

### 8. Flexible event handling

The app supports different event types, including:

- Timed events
- All-day events
- Multi-day events
- Recurring events

This makes it usable for both detailed scheduling and higher-level planning.

### 9. Goal customization

Goals can be refined with details like:

- Notes
- Status
- Targets
- Colors
- Icons

This helps users make goals clearer and easier to recognize visually.

## Why It Feels Different

Most calendars help users remember what is scheduled.

Waydots helps users understand:

- what they planned
- what they actually spent time on
- which goals received attention
- whether their routine is building momentum

That is the core difference.

## Homepage Writing Direction

When generating homepage copy, emphasize:

- turning plans into measurable progress
- connecting calendar time with real goals
- building routines and consistency
- seeing where time actually goes
- clarity over chaos

Avoid describing the product like a generic scheduling app. It should feel like a calendar for intentional living, focused work, and goal momentum.
